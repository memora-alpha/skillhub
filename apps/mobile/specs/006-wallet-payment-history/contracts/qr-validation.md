# QR preparation and validation specification

**T038 PREP only; QR-01 BLOCKED.** No encoder, rendered matrix, independent decoder or real merchant payload exists in this increment. T042 remains unchecked. `tests/wallet-qr.test.ts` runs presentation-model assertions only, with zero skipped tests; its green result does not execute or pass the blocked rows below.

## Current synthetic evidence

- Safe fixtures: ASCII `synthetic-only-0001`, raw leading/trailing whitespace and CR/LF with Vietnamese/Euro/emoji, and a 4,112-character dense synthetic string. They are not bank or payOS payment payloads.
- Model retains raw text byte-for-byte as a JavaScript string; no normalization, trimming, URL/XML interpretation or external requests. Empty data is unavailable. Readiness/render-error/closed states are independent of a financial outcome.
- Render-error/retry/close retain the same synthetic payment identity. Close drops raw data; session/component disposal erases identity and payload. Retry changes presentation only and never invokes creation. The shell does not display raw text or pretend that its placeholder is scannable.
- L validates the state model; W may validate copy/actions/layout with an inert injected model. Neither establishes QR readability, byte encoding, gateway acceptance or atomic posting.
