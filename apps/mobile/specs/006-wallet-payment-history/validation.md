- Observer claims its pending promise synchronously before notifying subscribers, coalescing reentrant/manual calls. A current/focus/ticket/signal check runs both before the injected reader and after await. Pause/blur/background invalidate and abort the check, settle the local wait and ignore uncooperative late replies. Resume alone does not dispatch; configured preview focus/foreground may explicitly read the same known synthetic identity. No scheduled polling, SSE, timer budget, create/verify/replay or global HTTP behavior is introduced.
- With no accepted server freshness contract, a changed observation cannot replace the previous result merely because its request finished later. Independent preview dataset selection resets the observer and is explicitly labeled as different synthetic data, not a legal transition. Actual authoritative transition/freshness binding awaits GAP-05.
